Features

Everything built in. Nothing to install.

No extensions, no add-ons, no account. Every demo below is live, so go ahead and press things.

Canvas

Ask with a question mark.

Type ? and a question in the address bar and Apogee builds a board: a straight answer, facts with their sources, diagrams and small simulations you can play with. Follow-ups land on the same board, and ⇧↩ turns any search into one.

  • The answer in about a second, the full board in five to eight
  • Real photos from Wikimedia Commons, never generated ones
  • Boards disappear when you close them

Max and the agent

Max reads the page. The agent does the clicking.

Press ⌘J and ask about anything on screen, long videos included. Ask it to do something and the agent takes over the tab: it searches, clicks and types on its own, then stops and asks before payments, passwords, purchases, messages and deleting anything. esc stops it at any time.

  • A separate conversation for every tab
  • Pick its name, personality and color
  • Runs on Google Gemini with your own free key

Ad blocking

Ads and trackers, gone.

234,000 rules from 15 lists run inside WebKit itself, so ads, trackers and app telemetry are stopped before a page even asks for them. YouTube gets its own blocker for the ads that lists can't catch. One click on the shield turns blocking off for a single site.

  • EasyList, EasyPrivacy, AdGuard, Peter Lowe and more
  • Lists update by themselves once a week
  • Pages load lighter, so they load faster

YouTube

YouTube, without the noise.

No ads, sponsor segments skipped with SponsorBlock, the highest quality picked on every video and ambient light that spills the colors of the video around the player. YouTube also stays pitch black, the way it should look on a dark Mac.

  • Picture in picture for any video
  • Ask Max to sum up a video from its captions
  • Full screen video that fills the whole screen

Passwords

Passwords that stay on your Mac.

A vault in your macOS Keychain that fills logins, offers to save new ones after you sign in and makes strong passwords for new accounts. Bring your passwords and sign-ins from Chrome or Brave, import a CSV from the Passwords app, or export one back whenever you like.

  • Fills only on the exact site the password belongs to
  • Never for this site, when you'd rather it didn't ask
  • Nothing leaves your Mac

Voice

Talk to it. Out loud.

Hold ⇧⌘D to dictate, or press ⇧⌘L for a live conversation with Max. It answers with a voice that fits its personality, cancels its own echo and lets you cut in mid-sentence. Every word lands in the chat as captions.

  • Understands the language your Mac is set to
  • Live voice connects in about a second
  • Hand it a task and the agent takes it from there

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download the DMG for your Mac and open it.
  2. Drag Apogee into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it the long way, once. Apogee isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch shows a warning. Here's how past it:
    macOS 15 Sequoia and newer

    Close the warning. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password and Apogee opens.

    macOS 14 Sonoma

    Right-click Apogee in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog.

  4. Add an AI key if you want Max, the agent, canvas and voice. Get a free Gemini key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it in Settings, AI keys (⌘,). It stays in your Keychain and goes only to Google. Add a few and Apogee moves to the next one when a key hits its limit. Everything else works without one.
